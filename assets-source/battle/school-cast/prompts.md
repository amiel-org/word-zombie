# School zombie cast prompts

Shared art direction for every character:

- Original full-body 2D game character illustration, schoolyard horror-comedy tone.
- Painterly storybook rendering with crisp silhouette and detailed fabric texture.
- Muted gray-green zombie skin; deep forest green, burgundy, navy, mustard and warm cream clothing palette.
- Walking toward screen-left, head turned slightly toward the viewer, arms reaching toward screen-left.
- Transparent background, centered character, feet fully visible, no ground shadow.
- No words, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, frame, UI, weapon, blood or gore.
- Must look like a genuinely different person and silhouette rather than a recolor of another character.

## Female campus zombie

Tall young adult female zombie with a high side ponytail and loose strands, long burgundy cardigan, cream blouse, calf-length asymmetric skirt, striped socks, worn school satchel bouncing behind her. Narrow upper body, long stride, one arm extended and the other holding a closed notebook with no writing. Clearly feminine face and silhouette without glamour posing.

## General teacher zombie

Lean middle-aged male teacher zombie with swept-back hair, long dark-green teaching coat over a cream shirt, rolled sleeves, narrow trousers and worn leather shoes. Carrying a thick stack of blank lesson papers under one arm and a plain wooden pointer in the other. Tall angular silhouette, bent shoulders, slow dragging step.

## Math teacher zombie

Lanky older male math teacher zombie with oversized square glasses, chalk-stained mustard sweater vest, white shirt, bow tie, high-waisted dark trousers and mismatched shoes. Holding a large plain wooden triangular ruler and a metal drawing compass, both without markings. Very angular elbows and knees, nervous shuffling pose, faint chalk dust only around his clothes.

## PE teacher zombie

Broad-shouldered stocky adult PE teacher zombie wearing a retro navy-and-cream tracksuit with burgundy stripes, whistle on a blank cord and stopwatch with no readable display. Muscular legs, one knee lifted in an exaggerated running step, elbows pumping, confident athletic silhouette. Must look like an adult coach, not the existing young athlete zombie.

## Discipline director zombie

Very round heavy middle-aged school discipline director zombie with a wide torso and short sturdy legs, dark burgundy vest over a strained cream shirt, loose tie, dark trousers and polished shoes. Clutching a thick blank clipboard under one arm and reaching forward with the other, stern eyebrows, heavy stomping stride. Strong circular silhouette, humorous but not grotesque.
